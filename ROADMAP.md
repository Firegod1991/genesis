# Roadmap

What is planned, roughly in order. Issues and pull requests against any of it are welcome.

## Next

* **Proof on real hardware.** Raspberry Pi 5 install, an NVIDIA GPU, and each radio profile with a real receiver, written up in the docs.
* **Routing in the dashboard.** Directions from your position to the nearest water or hospital using the Valhalla engine that the `routing` profile already starts.
* **Search across the vault.** One search box over Kiwix books, scripture, documents and survival layers.
* **Pick what to download from the dashboard.** Tick the shelves you want, see what they cost in disk space and watch them come down, with no command to type.
* **Hotspot and captive portal** set up by `genesis hotspot`, so any phone that joins lands on the dashboard.
* **Offline callsign lookup** in the dashboard from the FCC database the radio pack already carries.
* **Season aware shortwave schedules** and satellite pass prediction from the stored orbits.

## Later

* Terrain and contour lines for the maps.
* Voice: speech to text and text to speech through the Whisper models already in the catalog.
* A phone app, in its own repository and in development: choose what to keep on the phone, offline maps that follow your GPS, and the radio pages. Until then every phone on the box's network uses Genesis in its browser, from a code on the home page, and keeps it on the home screen.
* Package mirrors (apt, PyPI, npm) so software can still be installed and built.
* Printable field cards generated from the survival layers for a chosen area.
* More scripture study tools: parallel reading across translations, and original language lexicons where the license allows.
* Translations of the dashboard itself.

## Not planned

* Anything that needs an account, a subscription or a cloud service.
* Bundling copyrighted material without permission.
* Weapons manufacturing content.
