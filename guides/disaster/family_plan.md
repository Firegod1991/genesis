# Family emergency plan

Phones go down in a disaster, and the people you need are rarely in the same place when it hits. A plan agreed on beforehand, on paper, is what brings them back together. Fill this in with the whole household, keep a printed copy in every go bag and in the car, and put a copy of the filled in plan in the Safe on this drive. Practice it once a year.

## How we reach each other: PACE

Plan four ways, in order. If one fails, move to the next without waiting to talk about it.

* **Primary:** phone call. Numbers for everyone:
* **Alternate:** text message. Texts often get through when calls do not.
* **Contingency:** an **out of area contact,** a relative far away. Everyone calls or texts that one person, who passes the news on. Name and number:
* **Emergency:** radio. Our channel, and when we listen (for example, five minutes on every hour): see the radio card on this shelf.

Write the numbers on paper. Nobody remembers numbers anymore, and a dead phone takes them with it.

## What is in a go bag

Enough for three days for each person:

* Water, a gallon a day each, and a way to make more safe
* Food that needs no cooking, and a can opener
* A flashlight, a radio that runs on batteries or a crank, spare batteries
* First aid kit and a tourniquet, and a week of every medicine
* Copies of papers, some cash in small bills
* A change of clothes, sturdy shoes, a rain layer, a blanket
* A whistle, a map of the area on paper, and a phone charger
* For babies, the old and pets: what each one needs
