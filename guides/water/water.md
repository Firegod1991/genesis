# Water: find it, make it safe, store it

A person can live about three weeks without food and about three days without water, less in heat. Dirty water is the other way it kills: diarrhea from bad water drains a body faster than thirst. Make every drink safe.

The full guides from the EPA and the World Health Organization are on the same shelf.

## How much

* Store at least **one gallon a person a day,** half to drink and half for cooking and washing. More for heat, nursing mothers, the sick and children.
* Keep at least two weeks if you can. Count pets.

## Where to find it

* **Already in the house:** the water heater tank (turn off its power or gas first, then open the drain at the bottom), the pipes (open the highest tap, then drain from the lowest), ice cubes, and the tank of the toilet, not the bowl, if no cleaner is in it.
* **Rain:** catch it off a roof or a tarp into clean containers. Throw away the first rain off a dirty roof.
* **On the land:** go downhill. Look for green plants in dry country, birds flying low and straight at dawn and dusk, and animal trails that come together. In a dry streambed, dig at the outside of a bend, where water lingers longest.
* **Dew:** drag a clean cloth through wet grass at dawn and wring it out.

Treat it all, even water that looks clean. Clear water carries germs too.

## Make it safe

### 1. Clear it first

Cloudy water stops every method below from working well. Let it settle, then pour off the top through a clean cloth, a coffee filter or a towel.

### 2. Boil it: the surest way

Bring it to a **rolling boil for one minute.** Above 6,500 feet, boil it for **three minutes.** Let it cool covered. Pour it from one clean container to another a few times to bring back the taste.

### 3. Or bleach

Plain, **unscented** household bleach, the amounts the EPA gives, nothing with "splash free" or scent. Check the label for how strong it is.

| Water | Bleach of 6% | Bleach of 8.25% |
|---|---|---|
| 1 quart or 1 liter | 2 drops | 2 drops |
| 1 gallon | 8 drops | 6 drops |
| 2 gallons | 16 drops (1/4 teaspoon) | 12 drops (1/8 teaspoon) |
| 4 gallons | 1/3 teaspoon | 1/4 teaspoon |
| 8 gallons | 2/3 teaspoon | 1/2 teaspoon |

* **Double the dose** if the water is cloudy, colored or very cold.
* Stir and **wait 30 minutes.** It should smell faintly of chlorine. If it does not, add the same dose again and wait 15 minutes more.
* Bleach loses strength with age. A bottle older than about a year may not be strong enough: use more, or boil instead.
* Bleach does not reliably kill **Cryptosporidium,** a germ common in streams. Boiling does.

### 4. Or tablets

Chlorine dioxide or iodine tablets from an outdoor store. Follow the label exactly, including the waiting time, which can be four hours in cold water. Iodine is not for pregnant women, people with thyroid trouble, or use for weeks on end.

### 5. Or a filter

A filter for camping removes bacteria and parasites. Most do **not** remove viruses. Where people or sewage are upstream, filter, then add bleach or a tablet as well.

### 6. Or the sun, when nothing else is left

Fill clear plastic bottles, two liters or smaller, with clear water. Lay them on their side in full sun for **six hours,** or **two days** if the sky is cloudy. It does not work with cloudy water, glass, or colored bottles.

## Never drink

* **Seawater** or other salty water. It makes you more dehydrated, not less.
* **Urine.**
* **Floodwater** or water near a crash, a factory, a mine or farm runoff. Boiling kills germs but does not take out fuel, chemicals or metals.
* Water from a **car radiator.** Antifreeze is poison.
* **Alcohol** in place of water.

## Store it

* Food grade containers only, washed and rinsed with a little bleach water. Never ones that held milk, fuel or chemicals.
* Cool, dark and off concrete.
* Bought bottled water: keep it to its date. Water you bottled yourself: change it every six months.

## Signs of too little water

Dark urine or none, headache, dizziness, dry mouth, confusion. In a child: no tears, a sunken soft spot on a baby's head, very sleepy. For diarrhea, give water with a little salt and sugar: half a teaspoon of salt and six level teaspoons of sugar in one liter of safe water, sipped often.
