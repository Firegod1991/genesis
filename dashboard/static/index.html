    Safe

    A locked place on the drive for your own papers: identity papers, deeds, medical records, pictures of what cannot be replaced. One passphrase opens it, and nobody can recover that passphrase for you.

      The safe opens on the box itself and never through this page, so its passphrase is typed on the box and nowhere else. Open, your papers are an ordinary folder kept in memory. Lock the safe before you switch off, or what you changed is lost. On the drive it is one file, files/safe/safe.7z, which any computer with 7-Zip opens with the same passphrase.

      Systems to install

      Whole operating systems. Write one to a USB stick to bring a dead computer back. On the box, sudo genesis bootstick makes a stick that starts a PC from these, and sudo genesis bootstick --pi writes a spare card for a Raspberry Pi.
